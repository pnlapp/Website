PnL App vs TraderSync

TraderSync solved the data entry problem. PnL App is built to solve the problem that's usually still there even after the data entry is solved.

Short answer

TraderSync's biggest strength is automated trade import from most major brokers, which removes the manual logging friction that causes a lot of traders to quit journaling within a few weeks. PnL App also aims to minimize that friction, but its core focus is different: capturing the emotional state and behavior around each trade, and intervening on risky patterns in real time, not just recording what happened.

01 Where they differ

What gets captured, and what happens with it

  1. TraderSync leans into automation for data. Auto-importing trades from a connected broker means the P&L and trade details are logged automatically, which is a real advantage for high-frequency traders who would otherwise skip logging out of sheer volume.
  2. PnL App leans into automation for behavior. Alongside trade data, it's built to capture the context that explains a trade, emotional state, whether a rule was followed, how size compared to plan, and act on risky patterns as they emerge.
  3. Reporting philosophy differs. TraderSync's reports are built to answer "what happened in my trading." PnL App's reports are built to answer "why did it happen," which is a different question with a different kind of answer.
  4. Neither replaces a trading plan. Both tools work best paired with clear entry, exit, and sizing rules already in place, they measure adherence to a plan rather than creating one for you.
02 Who each one fits

Matching the tool to the actual problem

A practical way to decide

If the main reason you don't journal is the friction of manual data entry across many trades a day, TraderSync's broker automation directly solves that. If you already have your trade data but keep repeating the same emotional mistakes without seeing the pattern clearly, PnL App's behavioral layer is built for that gap specifically.

“Automating the data entry doesn't automatically explain the why behind a losing trade.”
