00 Investing Idea

The psychology of artificial intelligence

The strategy itself is straightforward. The behavior around it is where most of the actual risk lives.

Short answer

AI-related stocks have produced some of the sharpest sentiment swings of any recent theme, which means the discipline risk isn't missing the trend, it's sizing individual positions as if the whole sector's excitement applies equally to every name in it.

01 The behavioral risk

Sector enthusiasm doesn't distribute evenly

A rising theme lifts some names on real fundamentals and others purely on association, and the gap between the two only becomes obvious after a correction separates them. Treating every AI-adjacent name as equally justified by the theme is a common way a real trend still produces avoidable individual losses.

02 What this looks like in practice

The pattern to watch for

Composite example

A basket of AI-adjacent names gets bought on the same thesis without differentiating between companies with real revenue exposure and companies riding sentiment alone. The correction that follows hits the weaker names far harder, and the portfolio wasn't sized to reflect that difference in advance.

“Not every name in a real trend deserves the same conviction.”

The strategy isn't the risk

Your behavior around it is.

PnL App tracks the decisions you make around every position, not just the position itself, so the pattern shows up before it costs you.
