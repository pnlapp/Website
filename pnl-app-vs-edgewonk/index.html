PnL App vs Edgewonk

Two trading journals built around different bets: one that deep custom statistics are what traders are missing, and one that real-time behavioral intervention is.

Short answer

Edgewonk is one of the original dedicated trading journals, strong on custom statistics, strategy tagging, and a trading coach feature that flags patterns in your data after you log them. PnL App takes a different approach: mobile-first, built to capture emotional state alongside every trade and intervene in real time, before a risky pattern turns into a losing session, not only report on it afterward.

01 Where they differ

Depth of stats vs. speed of intervention

  1. Edgewonk's strength is customization. Its statistics engine lets a trader slice performance by almost any variable, which rewards traders who already know exactly what they want to measure and are comfortable with a more spreadsheet-like interface.
  2. PnL App's strength is timing. Instead of surfacing a pattern the next time you open a report, it aims to catch a risky moment, like sizing up right after a loss, while it's still happening, through features like a pre-trade gate and discipline mode.
  3. Platform shapes the workflow. Edgewonk is built around a desktop-first review session. PnL App is mobile-first, designed to be logged into and checked throughout a trading day, not just during a dedicated end-of-day review.
  4. Both require honest logging. Neither tool works if the underlying trade and behavioral data isn't entered consistently, the difference is in what each one does with that data once it exists.
02 Who each one fits

Matching the tool to the actual problem

A practical way to decide

If your losing trades are usually explained by a strategy or setup issue you want to analyze in detail, Edgewonk's depth of customization is built for exactly that. If your losing trades are more often explained by what you were feeling or doing right before you clicked buy, PnL App's real-time behavioral layer is built for that instead.

“One tool explains what happened in detail. The other tries to catch it before it happens.”
