PnL App Editorial standards

Useful before persuasive.

Last updated: August 2026

PnL App publishes educational content about trading psychology, trading journals, and decision-making. It is not investment research, personalized financial advice, or a promise of trading results.

How we create content

Our team develops articles from product knowledge, publicly available primary sources, and established educational material. When a guide refers to exchange hours, calendars, or policy events, we link to the official source where practical. We aim to describe uncertainty plainly instead of presenting market observations as guarantees.

Reviews and comparisons

Comparison pages explain PnL App’s perspective and may contain links to competing products. Product features, pricing, and availability can change; readers should verify current details directly with each provider before choosing a tool. We do not accept payment to alter a comparison’s conclusion.

Corrections and updates

We review content when a material factual issue is reported or a referenced source changes. Send correction requests to hello@pnlapp.co with the page URL, the statement in question, and supporting source material.

Our data and research

We will label any future analysis based on PnL App data as our own, explain its methodology, and distinguish it from independent research. We do not present internal observations as representative of all traders unless the evidence supports that conclusion.
