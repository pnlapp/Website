00 Feature

Portfolio tracker

Most trackers show you what you hold. This one shows you what you hold and the state of mind you were in when you decided to hold it.

Short answer

PnL App's portfolio tracker keeps a running view of your open and closed positions, automatically updated as you log trades, and pairs that with the behavioral data PnL App already captures, emotional state, rule adherence, sizing versus plan, so you're never looking at performance without the context that explains it.

01 Why it's different

Numbers without context are half the picture

A standalone portfolio tracker can tell you that a position is up or down. It can't tell you whether it was sized according to your plan, whether it was entered on a valid setup, or whether the exit was disciplined or emotional. PnL App's tracker is built inside the same app that captures that context, so the two never have to be reconciled by hand.

See your positions and your patterns together

One view. Both halves of the picture.

Download PnL App to track your open positions and the behavior behind every one of them, in the same place.
