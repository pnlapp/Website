Which trading journal fits the way you actually trade?

A journal is only useful if it changes your next decision. This side-by-side comparison makes the core workflow of each platform clear: performance analysis, trade review, or the behavioral habits behind your trades.

Questions worth asking

  • Can you capture the context behind a trade—not only its P&L?
  • Does the review view make recurring mistakes easy to identify?
  • Can you maintain the process during a busy trading week?
  • Are the markets, brokers, devices, and pricing right for your actual setup?
