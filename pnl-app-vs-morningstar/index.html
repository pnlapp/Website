PnL App vs Morningstar

One question worth asking honestly first: are these even solving the same problem? Mostly, no, and that's the actual answer.

Short answer

Morningstar is built for researching and rating funds and stocks before you buy them, star ratings, analyst reports, fair value estimates. PnL App is built for what happens after you've already decided to trade, tracking the behavior and emotional pattern behind your actual decisions. They're not direct competitors so much as tools that sit at different stages of the same process.

01 Where they actually differ

Research before the trade vs. behavior during and after it

  1. Morningstar answers "what should I buy." Its research, ratings, and analyst estimates are built to inform the decision of what to put in a portfolio in the first place.
  2. PnL App answers "why did I actually do that." Once a trade is placed, PnL App's focus is on capturing the emotional state, rule adherence, and sizing decisions behind it, the part no research platform tracks.
  3. Using both isn't redundant. A trader can do their research on Morningstar and still repeatedly override their own plan out of fear or FOMO once the position is live, which is exactly the gap PnL App is built to close.
Good research still needs disciplined execution

Research tells you what. PnL App tracks how.

Pair your research process with a behavioral layer that catches the decisions research alone can't predict.
