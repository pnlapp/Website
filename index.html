04 Meet Pnlee

Your journal says you lost. Pnlee tells you why.

Pnlee is the behavioral mentor inside PnL. It doesn't sell signals or predict the market. It reviews your trading history to help you spot a pattern and choose what to do next.

Facts first, support second. If your records show revenge trading, Pnlee points it out calmly and without judgment.
Process over outcome. A winning trade can still be a bad trade. A losing trade can still be clean.
Protective when it counts. After a loss, Pnlee blocks the recovery trade and starts a cooldown.
Speaks your language. Coaching in the words you actually use.
