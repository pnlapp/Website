04 Meet Pnlee

Your journal says you lost. Pnlee tells you why.

Pnlee is the behavioral mentor inside PnL. It doesn't sell signals or predict the market — it diagnoses the trader first, turning your history into one clear insight and one next action.

  • Facts first, support second. If the data shows revenge trading, Pnlee says it — calmly, without shame.
  • Process over outcome. A winning trade can still be a bad trade. A losing trade can still be clean.
  • Protective when it counts. After a loss, Pnlee blocks the recovery trade and starts a cooldown.
  • Speaks your language. Coaching that meets you where you are — in the words you actually use.
