PnL App vs Tradervue

Tradervue built its reputation on reporting depth. PnL App is built around a different question: what happens if you catch the pattern before the report even needs to be run.

Short answer

Tradervue is one of the longer-standing trading journals, known for dense, highly configurable reporting that's particularly popular with options and futures traders who want granular statistics. PnL App takes a different approach, focused on capturing emotional state alongside trade data and intervening on risky behavior in real time, rather than primarily reporting on it after the fact.

01 Where they differ

Reporting depth vs. real-time intervention

  1. Tradervue's strength is configurability. Its reporting is dense and flexible enough to satisfy traders who want a specific number sliced a specific way, which appeals especially to more experienced options and futures traders.
  2. PnL App's strength is what happens while you're trading. Rather than waiting for a scheduled review to surface a problem, features like a pre-trade gate and discipline mode are built to interrupt a risky pattern, like sizing up after a loss, in the moment it's happening.
  3. Different primary use case. Tradervue tends to get used in a dedicated end-of-day or end-of-week review session. PnL App is designed to be part of the trading day itself, not just the review of it.
  4. Reporting still matters either way. PnL App also gives behavioral reports over time, the difference is in when the tool acts, not whether reporting exists at all.
02 Who each one fits

Matching the tool to the actual problem

A practical way to decide

If you're comfortable identifying your own behavioral patterns from detailed statistics and mainly want a powerful reporting engine, Tradervue's configurability is a strong fit. If you'd rather have a pattern flagged for you as it's forming, before it shows up as a number in a report, PnL App's real-time layer is built for that instead.

“A great report on Friday doesn't undo a bad decision made on Tuesday.”

Catch the pattern while it's forming

Not just a report, a warning.

PnL App flags risky behavioral patterns in real time, so the correction happens before the losing trade, not after the losing week.
