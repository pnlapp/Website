01 Why this matters

The ratio means nothing without the win rate

A 1:3 risk/reward ratio sounds great until you realize a strategy with that ratio still loses money overall if it wins less than 25% of the time. The ratio and your actual, logged win rate always have to be read together. Neither one alone tells you whether a strategy works.
