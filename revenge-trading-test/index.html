Are you revenge trading?

Six questions. No signup required to see your score. Find out if your next trade is a setup — or a reaction.

Short answer

Revenge trading is opening a new position to emotionally recover a previous loss, rather than because a valid setup appeared. If you increase size, skip your checklist, or re-enter the same symbol within minutes of a loss, you're showing the pattern. The test below scores how strongly.

01 The test

Answer honestly. It only scores you.

1. After a loss, do you feel an urge to immediately open a new trade to "make it back"?
2. Do you ever increase your position size right after a losing trade?
3. Have you re-entered the same symbol within 30 minutes of a stop-out?
4. Do you skip your entry checklist when you're trying to recover a loss?
5. Does a losing streak make you trade more often, not less?
6. Have you moved your stop loss wider mid-trade to "give it room" after already being down?

03 A real pattern

What this looks like on an actual account

Composite example, from PnL App usage patterns

Down $180 on a clean EUR/USD short that hit its stop. Eleven minutes later, a new long on the same pair, 1.6× the usual size, no journal entry, no checklist. It lost too. Forty minutes after that, a third trade — smaller this time, more careful, and it worked. Three trades, one loss that mattered, and two that were never really about the chart.
