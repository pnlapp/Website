00 Feature

Market insights

Not predictions about the market. Insights about you, built from your own trade history.

Short answer

Pnlee, the AI behavioral mentor inside PnL App, doesn't sell signals or forecast prices. It studies your behavior, time of day, emotional notes, sizing patterns, and turns your own trade history into one clear insight and one next action, growing sharper the more you trade.

01 Why insights, not predictions

The market doesn't need your permission. Your behavior does

A prediction about where a market is headed is a guess dressed up as insight. A pattern pulled from your own trade history, like "you increase size within 20 minutes of a loss," is verifiable and actionable in a way a market call never is. Pnlee is built around the second kind of insight specifically.
